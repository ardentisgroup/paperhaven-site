Paperhaven Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026 Developer: Artendis Contact: ardentis.group@gmail.com

In short

Paperhaven works on your device. We, Artendis, do not collect, receive, store or share your documents or your personal data. There is no account and no analytics in the app. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; the ad SDK never receives your documents. Paperhaven Pro removes the ads.

Your documents

  • Files you open, scan or create are kept in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Everything the app does with them happens on your device: reading, text recognition (OCR), search, compressing, signing, merging and editing pages. Your documents are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Deleting a document in the app, or deleting the app, removes the files from the app's storage.
  • Device backups: if you have turned on your phone's own backup (iCloud Backup on iPhone, Google backup on Android), the operating system may include the app's files in that backup, stored in your Apple or Google account under their terms. We have no access to these backups.

Signatures and passwords

  • Signatures you choose to keep are stored only on your device.
  • Passwords you enter to open a protected PDF are kept in memory only while the app is open. They are never written to storage or sent anywhere.
  • The text of password-protected PDFs is not stored for search.

Camera and photos

The camera and your photo library are used only when you scan a document or choose photos to add. The images stay on your device.

Data we collect

Artendis collects nothing: there are no analytics in the app and we do not operate servers that receive data from it. The third-party components below receive the data they describe.

Ads (free version)

The free version shows a banner and, at most once an hour, a full-screen ad when the app opens. They come from Google AdMob. To choose and measure ads, Google's SDK collects device information: the advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device model and language, and how you interact with the ads (Google's disclosure). It does not receive your documents, file names, recognised text or signatures.

  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, with Google's consent form. Without consent you see non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • On iPhone, personalised ads use the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when asked.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.
  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

With Paperhaven Pro no ads are requested or shown.

Purchases

Paperhaven Pro is sold through Google Play and the App Store. Payment is handled entirely by Google or Apple; Artendis receives no payment details. The app asks the store only whether your account owns Pro and keeps that answer on your device.

Third-party components

  • Android: Paperhaven uses Google ML Kit for text recognition and Google Play services for the document scanner. Text recognition runs on your device. Google's ML Kit SDK sends limited technical diagnostics to Google: device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), app information (package name, version), a per-installation identifier, performance metrics, API configuration, event types and error codes. Google states that this data is used for diagnostics and usage analytics, is encrypted in transit and is not transferred to third parties (Google's disclosure). Paperhaven does not send your documents, images or recognised text to Google.
  • iPhone and iPad: Paperhaven uses Apple's built-in frameworks (Vision, VisionKit, PDFKit), which run on your device.

Sharing

A document leaves the app only when you choose to share it; it then goes to the app or person you pick.

Children

Paperhaven is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: ardentis.group@gmail.com
